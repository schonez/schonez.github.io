Life OS – Privacy policy

Life OS is a private tool used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

With the owner's consent it reads the account's YouTube subscriptions and liked videos, and can create private playlists in that same account.

The data is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising.

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
